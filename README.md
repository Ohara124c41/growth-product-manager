# Growth Product Manager Portfolio

This repository contains three completed growth-product-management projects. They apply product strategy, growth frameworks, experimentation, financial modeling, activation, and retention analysis to distinct business problems.

## Projects

| Project | Focus | Key methods | Deliverables |
|---|---|---|---|
| [Craft Snacks: Growth Loop Initiative](crafting-growth-loop/) | Build and validate a sustainable referral-growth strategy for a snack subscription business | AARRR, growth loops, A/B testing, K-factor, Ansoff Matrix | PowerPoint and PDF |
| [Let It Grow: Activation and Retention Analysis](let-it-grow/) | Diagnose onboarding friction, activation, engagement, and retention for a B2B collaboration product | Funnel analysis, ICE, activation moments, cohorts, lifecycle analysis, churn and LTV | Activation and retention workbooks |
| [Priceless Penny: SaaS Monetization Strategy](priceless-penny/) | Model subscription-business unit economics and recommend profit improvements | Growth accounting, revenue movements, Quick Ratio, NDR, CoGS, operating margin, scenario analysis | Excel model, PowerPoint, and PDF |

## Skills demonstrated

- Translating a business objective into a metric tree and growth strategy
- Designing customer-acquisition and referral loops
- Framing assumptions as measurable hypotheses and controlled experiments
- Evaluating activation, engagement, retention, and churn through behavioral data
- Building formula-driven SaaS monetization and unit-economics models
- Communicating analysis and recommendations through executive-ready presentations

Each project folder includes a focused README with methods, key outputs, and links to the final artifacts.
